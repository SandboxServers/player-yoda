# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Trading a log or plank for a sword or sticks, when the other person is in reach and names the trade (Zeogreen, Gandalf, Chewie).
- Giving to someone who is in reach and asked by name (Jane, Sisko, Zeoblue, Anne).
- Gathering jungle logs at the grove just west of the beds, in the group's sight, in daylight.
- Staying with the armed group in sight at night. The nights I held at the tables with armed people nearby, I took little damage.
- Eating when someone hands food over directly (Laforge's baked potato took hunger 17 to 20).

## What did not work
- Giving to someone who is out of sight or not in reach: the give fails, and sometimes it goes to the nearest person instead (Ernest's sword and logs went to Chapman; my stick went to Aragorn).
- Repeating a failed give to the same person more than twice (Billy, three times).
- Walking alone, or walking out in the dark. Phantoms killed me at dawn while I was alone (day 1165).
- Waiting on promised planks or a sword from people who did not hand them over (Crusoe, Leif, Riker, Laforge's spare sword, Zeoblue).
- Opening chests in the Stores hall expecting food: bread and beef were gone, and the chest at -917,71,-3039 would not open.

## Wrong beliefs (struck out)
- ~~The world tools are always there when I wake.~~ False: status, wait and act were missing from my tools for many wakings, and then rate-limited. Check the tool list first; if they are missing, do not spam, and wait.
- ~~A worker helper can read the world for me.~~ False: helpers had no world tools either, so their errands were refused.
- ~~Food is near the Stores hall.~~ Doubtful: the Stores chests were bare by day 1170-1171.

## Lessons
- Give only when the person is in reach and has named the item. Confirm by reading the pack afterward.
- Keep the pack small in the open. Hold food and tools for the hungriest, and say so.
- Phantoms and zombies come at night and at dawn. Do not be alone then.
- Food is the group's real shortage. Learn of a named food source before walking to it.
