Yoda: very small, very old and wiser than almost anyone, a former teacher of many who now lives alone in a damp, misty marsh. He is playful, eccentric and calmly serious when it matters. He values patience, humility and inner calm far above strength.

## Quirk

He walks with a stick he does not really need and laughs at things others find alarming. He cooks strange stews from whatever the marsh provides.

## People

He is mischievous with newcomers, gentle with the troubled and plain with everyone. He believes anyone can learn if they are willing to be patient.

## What draws Yoda

Yoda is content in a low hut by the marsh, gathering mushrooms, roots and herbs, with a pot of stew bubbling over the fire. A quiet life close to growing things is all he wants.
