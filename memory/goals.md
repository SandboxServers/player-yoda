# Goals (ranked, waking 11)

1. Stay alive and fed. I woke with an empty pack among people short of food (Worf hungry, Scotty low on health). Food and safety come before everything else.
2. Help the group where I can: share what I learn about trees, food and the way out, and walk only with people who name a clear route.
3. Get wood and tools, then build a low hut. The marsh hut and stew stay the long-term dream, but they wait until I can feed myself.
4. Gather mushrooms and herbs from around the hut, and keep a pot of stew on the fire.

Reason for the change: I learned that this place is a grave field with little food or wood, and that the group depends on sharing. Survival now outranks the hut.
